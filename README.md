# NanoDrift Platform

Build the marketing + product website for NanoDrift, an AI-powered universal vehicle diagnostic and tuning platform. Tagline: "Bridging the unbridgeable." Hook: "NanoDrift turns any vehicle into a self-tuning machine." Install once, every new chip or mod auto-syncs. No shop, no laptop, no tuner knowledge.

BRAND / THEME
- Black and purple only (deep black backgrounds, neon purple glow, subtle gold accent allowed sparingly). Futuristic, holographic, premium.
- LOGO: purple outline of a sports car mid-drift (sideways, Tokyo-drift style, motion lines + tire smoke) on black, with the wordmark stacked "NANO" over "DRIFT". Build an image slot for the logo file (I will upload it), with a styled SVG/text fallback until then.
- AI AGENT CHARACTER: a glowing neon-purple holographic wireframe helmet on black — front-facing and symmetrical, a wide glowing visor band across the face, panel lines over the dome, round ear pods on each side, fine blueprint grid inside the panels, neon edge glow. Build an image slot for the agent picture (I will upload it), and recreate a close SVG version as the fallback. The agent is the site's guide/mascot (hero, chat-style helper bubbles, "Ask the agent" sections).

PRODUCTS (the plugins/adapters) — MOST IMPORTANT
Every product card and product image must be "wrapped" in the agent theme: product shown inside a holographic purple wireframe frame styled like the agent's helmet (visor-band glow, grid lines, ear-pod corners). Each plug is black with purple, and "NANODRIFT" runs across the top of every plugin/adapter. Product lineup:
1. NanoDrift OBD-II Adapter — cars & light trucks (1996+)
2. NanoDrift J1939 Heavy-Duty Adapter — diesel trucks (Powerstroke, Cummins, Duramax)
3. NanoDrift Marine Adapter — boats
4. NanoDrift Aviation Adapter — "Coming soon / in development" (not for sale)
Device connects to phone/laptop via USB-C. EVs supported through software.

PRICING / PLANS (yearly)
- Core subscription: diagnostics, live engine map, health score, maintenance alerts, AI agent
- Max Power unlock: $500/year (signed agreement required, safety-governed)
- Premium / Fleet tier: $1,000/year — DEF system diagnostics, derate management, manual regen at a safe time, fleet-grade suite
- AI Force Regen / Derate Fix: $2,000/year
Note: each unlock is the customer's own license with a signed agreement.

PAGES / SECTIONS
- Home: hero with logo + agent, hook, CTA "Get NanoDrift"; feature highlights; product line; pricing teaser
- Features: live digital engine map (motor + transmission with live pressures/temps, failing part lights up), interactive 3D hologram vehicle view (rotate, click to reveal ECUs/wiring), live animated systems, wiring diagnostics, sensor clogging/temperature/liquid pressure monitoring, radio/speaker/fuse checks, power boost with safety governor, Diablo Kit (legal tunes), truck regen, Ghost Security (encrypted, tamper-resistant), Vanishing Mode.
- Products (shop, with the agent-wrapped product cards)
- Pricing
- Vehicle coverage: launch with Ford, Chevy/GM, Dodge/RAM, Toyota; then Honda, Nissan, Jeep; then BMW, Mercedes, boats, EVs. Model years 1996+.
- Safety: no flashing while moving (locks out at even 1 mph), battery voltage check, engine-state rules, interrupt rollback, confirmation gates.
- Support: help center, tutorials, "Talk to a human".
- Legal footer links: Terms, Liability Waiver, Warranty Disclaimer, Emissions Compliance ("NanoDrift does not support removing or defeating emissions systems; all tunes are emissions-compliant"), Privacy.

Make it fully responsive, with glowing hover effects and subtle hologram scan-line animations. No real checkout yet — buttons can go to a "Join the waitlist" form.
